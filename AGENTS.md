- Milestone 1 covers read-only discovery, source asset preservation and planning. Start application work, deployment setup or production changes only when explicitly requested for a later milestone.
- Read the [brand audit](docs/BRAND_AUDIT.md), [asset manifest](docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.md), [design system](docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) and [implementation plan](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) before changing scope, brand presentation or technical direction.
- Treat published site claims as evidence of existing copy. Use owner-approved facts for product specifications, availability, pricing, services, contact channels and social URLs. Keep marketing and private concierge consent separate.
- Preserve the original logo, established palette and Latin typography. Record the source and rights for each asset. Keep source files intact and store derivatives separately.
- For source discovery, check crawl rules per origin, follow published links, fetch serially at a modest rate, and pause on blocking or throttling. Review production forms without submitting data.
- When implementation is authorized, prioritize English and Arabic parity, semantic RTL, keyboard and screen-reader access, reduced motion, and verified SEO migration. Validate changes in both locales on mobile and desktop.
